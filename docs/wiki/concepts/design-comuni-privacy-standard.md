# Design Comuni: Privacy Step Standard

Standardized requirements for the "Informativa Privacy" step in municipal digital services, based on the Italian Digital Transformation Department guidelines.

## Required Components
- **Progressive Stepper:** Indicates the user's position in the flow (e.g., "Step 1 of 4").
- **Institutional Context:** Breadcrumbs and standard municipal header.
- **Legal Compliance:** Explicit reference to GDPR (Regulation UE 2016/679).
- **Interactive Checkbox:** Mandatory confirmation "Ho letto e compreso l'informativa sulla privacy".
- **Gated Navigation:** "Avanti" button is disabled until consent is checked.
- **Support Sidebar:** FAQs and contact information block.

## Design Philosophy
- **Clarity:** Institutional trust through clean typography and readable legal text.
- **Accessibility:** High contrast and standard HTML structures (W3C compliant).
