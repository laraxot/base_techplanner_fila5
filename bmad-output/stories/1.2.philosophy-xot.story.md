## Story

As a **documentation reader / future maintainer**,
I want a comprehensive `PHILOSOPHY.md` for the Xot module,
so that I understand the architectural religion, recommended packages, competitors, and installation of the Xot agnostic module.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. File `laravel/Modules/Xot/docs/PHILOSOPHY.md` opens with a YAML front matter block.
2. Front matter contains at minimum: `title`, `module`, `type`, `category`, `tags`, `last_updated`.
3. All 15 sections present: RELIGIONE, FILOSOFIA, POLITICA, SCOPO, ZEN, LIBRERIE, IMPLEMENTAZIONI FUTURE, MIGLIORAMENTI, DUBBI, COMPETITOR, ISPIRAZIONI, BUONE PRATICHE, CATTIVE PRATICHE, FALSE FRIENDS, COME USARLO, COME INSTALLARLO.
4. **COMPETITOR section explicitly analyzes `nwidart/laravel-modules`** (modular architecture) — listing what it does that Xot does not, and what Xot does that it does not.
5. **LIBRERIE section explicitly recommends `spatie/laravel-permission`** (RBAC) as a package to install, with justification.
6. No "FixCity" brand references (modules are agnostic).
7. Content is derived from existing `module.json`, real models (XotBaseModel, BaseModel, etc.), and existing docs.
