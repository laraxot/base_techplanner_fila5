# 1.1: Rewrite Cms docs/PHILOSOPHY.md

**Story ID:** 1.1
**Epic:** 1 — Rewrite 16 Module PHILOSOPHY.md

**Status:** ready-for-dev

> Owned by planning until `ready-for-dev`. Sized to one dev-day (~2-8h). No story points.

## Story

As a **documentation reader / future maintainer**,
I want a comprehensive `PHILOSOPHY.md` for the Cms module,
so that I understand the architectural religion, recommended packages, competitors, and installation of the Cms agnostic module.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. File `laravel/Modules/Cms/docs/PHILOSOPHY.md` opens with a YAML front matter block.
2. Front matter contains at minimum: `title`, `module`, `type`, `category`, `tags`, `last_updated`.
3. All 15 sections present: RELIGIONE, FILOSOFIA, POLITICA, SCOPO, ZEN, LIBRERIE, IMPLEMENTAZIONI FUTURE, MIGLIORAMENTI, DUBBI, COMPETITOR, ISPIRAZIONI, BUONE PRATICHE, CATTIVE PRATICHE, FALSE FRIENDS, INSTALLAZIONE.
4. **COMPETITOR section explicitly analyzes `z3d0x/filament-fabricator`** (block-based page builder) — listing what fabricator does that Cms does not, and what Cms does that fabricator does not.
5. **LIBRERIE section explicitly recommends `laravel/head`** (meta tags) as a package to install, with justification.
6. No "FixCity" brand references (modules are agnostic).
7. Content is derived from existing `module.json`, real models (Page, Section, Container, Block), and existing docs.
