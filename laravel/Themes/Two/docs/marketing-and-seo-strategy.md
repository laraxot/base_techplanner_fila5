# SEO & Inbound Marketing Strategy

## 1. SEO Objectives
- **Target Keywords**: Consulenza radioprotezione, esperto di radioprotezione, sicurezza radiologica D.Lgs 101/2020, controlli periodici RX, conformità Euratom.
- **Local SEO**: Focus on Italian regions and cities. Use GMB (Google My Business) synchronization and local landing pages.
- **Technical SEO**: 
  - Implementation of JSON-LD `LocalBusiness` and `ProfessionalService` schema.
  - 100% Core Web Vitals compliance.
  - Semantic HTML structure (H1 per page, proper H2-H6 hierarchy).

## 2. Inbound Marketing Plan
- **Educational Content**: 
  - Create a "Risorse" section with downloadable PDF guides (e.g., "Guida alla Conformità D.Lgs 101/2020").
  - Whitepapers for different sectors (Dental, Veterinary, Industrial).
- **Lead Generation**:
  - Clear CTAs ("Richiedi Preventivo", "Scarica la Guida").
  - Optimized contact forms with minimal friction.
- **Multilingual Support**:
  - Full SEO parity for IT and EN versions.
  - Hreflang tags implementation.

## 3. Adsense Integration
- Optimized placeholders for display ads in non-obtrusive areas (Blog sidebar, Footer).
- Ensure high-value content to maximize CPC (Cost Per Click) in the specialized safety niche.

## 4. Social Integration
- Professional LinkedIn presence.
- Direct links to social profiles with branded icons.
