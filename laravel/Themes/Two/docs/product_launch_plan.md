# Product Launch Plan - Theme Two

## Vue.js Frontend Theme

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Launch Date:** October 15, 2026 (Target - v1.0)  
**Launch Type:** Major Release (Alpha to v1.0)  
**Owner:** Theme Product Team

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the launch plan for Theme Two v1.0, marking the transition from alpha to production-ready status. The launch positions Theme Two as the Vue.js choice for Laravel applications, emphasizing SPA/PWA capabilities and Vue's component model.

### Launch Objectives

1. **Awareness:** Achieve 50% awareness among Vue/Laravel developers
2. **Adoption:** Secure 40 new projects within 90 days
3. **Engagement:** Grow community to 150+ members
4. **Validation:** Obtain 5+ case studies from early adopters

## Risk Management

### Launch Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Critical bug discovered | Medium | High | Rollback plan |
| Low initial adoption | Medium | Medium | Promotion boost |
| Negative feedback | Low | High | Rapid response |
| Infrastructure issues | Low | High | Redundancy |

### Contingency Plans

#### Critical Bug at Launch
1. Acknowledge within 1 hour
2. Deploy rollback if critical
3. Communicate fix timeline
4. Deploy hotfix within 24h
5. Follow-up communication

#### Low Webinar Registration
1. Activate partner network
2. Increase social frequency
3. Ensure recording available
